How to read this

  • Vacancy share: the percentage of vacancies that mention a skill, out of vacancies with at least one skill. Raw counts are not used, because ads list more skills every year.
  • Change is the difference in vacancy share between 2017–2019 and 2023–2025, in percentage points (hover it to see the ratio). A skill is Growing if its share rose by at least 50%, and Declining if it fell by at least a third. Rare skills are marked Few ads.
  • Skill groups come from the ESCO classification (language, transversal and digital lists, skill type), with manual corrections.
  • 2026 covers January to August only and is drawn as a dashed line.
  • Skills are detected automatically in the ad text. A change can come from the detection method, not only from employers.

Data: ADEM (Agence pour le développement de l'emploi), ESCO v1.2.1 (European Commission).
